There was once an underground civilization living deep below the surface. But they're all gone now.

What happened to them? Who knows. But one thing is certain: they left a lot of loot down there.

Build a tunnel leading to a vast network of forgotten dungeons and send your colonists scavenging for food, materials, weapons, apparel, medicine, drugs, and rare treasures.

## Supported RimWorld Versions

- 1.5
- 1.6
